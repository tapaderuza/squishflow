Squishflow

Focus should not take force.

Squishflow puts a soft body between you and the work. You say what you want to finish; it hands back blocks short enough to believe. While you sit with the tension, you push your thumb into Squishy and it pushes back.

Every body in the app can be earned with focused minutes. Pro is for people who would rather not wait, and for protecting more than three apps.

Android and iPhone, from one Kotlin codebase. Open source under Apache 2.0: github.com/tapaderuza/squishflow.
