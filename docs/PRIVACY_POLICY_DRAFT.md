# Privacy Policy — Squishflow (Draft)

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Squishflow is operated by Álvaro Passalacqua in Málaga, Spain. Privacy questions can be sent to `apaspar8@gmail.com`.

## Data processed

The free planner, missions, session history and reflections are stored locally on your device. Camera frames used by the optional movement feature are processed live on-device and are not stored or uploaded.

If you choose a Pro AI feature, the goal text you submit is sent securely to our AI processing service solely to generate the requested plan or review. Squishflow does not retain the raw goal after the response by default and does not use it to train models. Technical security logs may temporarily contain request metadata but not goal content.

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information needed to provide Pro access. Apple or Google process payments under their own privacy policies.

The initial release does not use third-party behavioral advertising or behavioral analytics. Squishflow does not sell personal data.

## Optional permissions

On Android, the optional Accessibility Service observes the foreground application package only to detect applications you selected for a conscious pause. It does not read or store screen content. On iOS, optional Family Controls are used with your authorization to protect selected apps and websites. Camera permission is used only for optional on-device movement recognition.

## Legal basis and choices

Local processing is necessary to provide requested app functionality. Optional AI processing, analytics if introduced later, camera and focus-protection permissions require a clear user choice. You may use the basic timer without enabling protection or camera features.

You can delete all locally stored Squishflow data from the app settings. Because the MVP has no account, Squishflow normally cannot identify or export data stored only on your device. Purchase records are managed through Apple, Google and RevenueCat.

## Age

Squishflow is intended for people aged 16 or older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## International processing and retention

AI and subscription providers may process data outside Spain under applicable safeguards. Raw AI goal text is discarded after completing the request by default. Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be explained in-app before taking effect. Contact `apaspar8@gmail.com` for privacy questions or requests.

This is a product draft and not legal advice. It must be reviewed and published at a stable public URL before release.
