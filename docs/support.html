Common questions

I paid and nothing unlocked. Open the plans screen and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, tell us which store and roughly when you bought it.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by the app: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Everything you earned stays after cancelling.

Protection is not working on Android. Check that Squishflow's accessibility service is on in Settings → Accessibility. Some phones switch it off after an update.

Protection on iPhone. The current iPhone build is a timer with the companion; per-app protection arrives with a later release.

Where is my data? On your phone, and nowhere else. See Privacy.

Bugs and ideas are also welcome as issues on GitHub.
