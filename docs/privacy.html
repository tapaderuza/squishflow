Privacy.

Last updated 12 September 2026

Squishflow is made by Álvaro Passalacqua in Málaga, Spain. Questions: apaspar8@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type and everything the app learns about you: your goals, the blocks it planned, how many minutes you have focused, which apps you chose to protect, which body you picked. None of it leaves the device. There is no account and no server of ours.

What leaves your phone

Only purchases. If you subscribe to Pro, RevenueCat processes the receipt and an anonymous app-user ID so the subscription can be recognised and restored. Google or Apple handle payment under their own policies. We never see your card.

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting that includes your content. This is true for paying and non-paying people alike.

Permissions

Android accessibility service (optional). Used only to notice when you open an app you chose to protect, so Squishy can offer a pause. It does not read, store or transmit anything on your screen. Turn it off in Settings at any time; the timer works without it.

Notifications. One, when a focus block you started has ended. Nothing else, ever, and only if you allow it.

Vibration. For the haptics when you press the body.

The app does not use the camera, the microphone, location or contacts.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, because nothing was stored anywhere else. Subscription records stay with Google, Apple and RevenueCat under their retention rules; you can ask us to request deletion of the anonymous RevenueCat record by email.

Age

Squishflow is for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
