## Description

Squishflow puts a soft body between you and the work.

Say what you want to finish. Squishflow hands back blocks short enough to believe, the first one shorter on purpose, because starting is the expensive part of focus. Then, while you sit with the tension, you push your thumb into Squishy and it pushes back.

Squishy is a physics model, not an animation. Press one side and the other bulges. Over a block it softens and its breathing slows, so a session visibly has depth instead of just a countdown. Leave it alone and it blinks, looks around, and occasionally stretches.

No guilt. An abandoned block is a thing that happened, not a verdict. Come back after a week away and the app says the gap does not count, because it does not.

FIVE BODIES, ALL EARNABLE
Jelly is free. Stress ball, Mochi, Water balloon and Bubble are earned with focused minutes, and each one genuinely feels and sounds different under your thumb. Pro unlocks them now for people who would rather not wait. Let Pro lapse and everything you earned stays.

PROTECT THE APPS THAT STEAL YOUR AFTERNOON
On Android, choose the apps you open on impulse. Opening one becomes a decision with Squishy in the middle. Free protects three apps; Pro protects every one.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
No account. No ads, for anyone. No analytics. Everything you type stays on your phone.

One Kotlin codebase, the same companion on Android and iPhone. Open source.
